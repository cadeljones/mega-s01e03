owner support desk active reviewed 2026-10-29 · 0 days ago

A busy morning brings reports with similar words but sometimes different causes. Keep one ticket per established cause, with an auditable count of distinct report IDs.

The rule

A report belongs with another when evidence supports the same cause (decision 0002, which supersedes the unchanged historical decision 0001):

  1. Keep one copy of each report ID; preserve the body, tenant, channel and timestamps.
  2. Resolve the tenant’s locale and booking-form version. For day-first tenants, booking-v3 form reports route to the hint issue; other supported paths route to the parser issue.
  3. Compare causes, not subjects or browser-based intake tags. Unknown tenant, unsupported locale, conflicting evidence or unexplained symptoms require investigation, not an automatic merge.
  4. Keep one issue per established cause, update its distinct-ID count, and verify each fix separately. See the #4471 split for issue links and unresolved reports.

Closure check: the course now reports #4471 closed on 5 October: parser fixed, hint won’t fix — booking-v3 form retired. Do not call retirement a repair. Before attaching a new report to either closed issue, check the current form/version and reproduce it. A post-fix report or use of a retired form needs investigation and a linked follow-up or reopening, not automatic merging into history.

Do not keep applying the old subject-only rule while the desk works. Matching words do not establish matching causes.

When you are unsure

Keep the report visible and unmerged until the cause is supported. An intake tag is a guess, and a calculated cause does not explain every unusual symptom. Investigate before closure.
